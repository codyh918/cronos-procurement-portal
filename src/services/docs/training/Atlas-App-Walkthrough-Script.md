# Atlas Procurement App Walkthrough Video Script

## Opening
Welcome to the Cronos Atlas Procurement app. This walkthrough shows how procurement team members use Atlas to manage projects, quotes, purchase orders, vendors, catalog records, tracking, and reports.

## 1. Sign In
Open the app and sign in using your Cronos email and password. Procurement users see only procurement tools. Admin users can manage users, roles, and settings.

## 2. Dashboard
The dashboard is your daily action queue. Review assigned tasks, due-outs, vendor updates, material risks, shipments, and alerts. Use filters to narrow the dashboard by user, project, customer, vendor, status, priority, or due date.

## 3. Projects
Projects are the center of the workflow. Open a project to view quotes, purchase orders, equipment, tracking, financial documents, and exports. Tracking can be updated directly from the project page, so the procurement team does not have to open every PO one by one.

## 4. Quotes
Create customer quotes from project line items. Enter manufacturer, part number, quantity, unit cost, description, lead time, and other quote details. Download Quote PDF or Quote Excel from the quote table. Once the customer approves the quote, mark it approved.

## 5. Purchase Orders
Approved quotes generate vendor purchase orders. Open purchase orders from inside the project without losing project context. Use Download PO PDF to create the Cronos-branded vendor purchase order.

## 6. Tracking
Update vendor order numbers, carriers, tracking numbers, estimated ship dates, and delivery information at the line-item level. Use the customer tracking report to share shipment status without exposing internal cost details.

## 7. Vendors and Catalog
Use Vendors to maintain vendor information and Catalog to support known parts and repeat purchasing. Atlas Assistant is available for quick workflow help.

## Admin Notes
Admins can manage users and settings. Project deletion is available from Edit Project, not from the project list, so accidental deletion is less likely.
